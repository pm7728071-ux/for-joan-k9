Proud of you

Four reasons I am proud of you

Read these on a good day. Read them twice on a hard one.

  1. Chapter One

    The Bravest Yes

    You said yes to work in a country where you knew no one. That is not a small thing, Joan. Plenty of people talk about courage; you bought the ticket and lived it.

  2. Chapter Two

    The Work Nobody Sees

    Running a home for someone else takes patience, organisation and heart, and you do it far from your own people. Every early morning you show up still counts, even on the days nobody says thank you.

  3. Chapter Three

    The Days That Feel Heavy

    Some days the distance wins and everything feels like too much. That does not mean you are failing, it means you are human and far from home. Feel it, then remember who you are and who is standing behind you.

  4. Chapter Four

    What You Are Building

    This chapter is not the whole story. It is the part that makes the rest possible: the plan, the savings, the woman who comes home knowing she can handle anything. Keep going, you are closer than it feels.

You Are Doing Better Than You Think

Joan, if you are reading this on a hard day, read it slowly. You are not stuck, you are in the middle, and the middle is always the least glamorous part of any story worth telling.

You left home to build something for yourself and for us, and you have done it with grace on days that would have broken other people. Being tired does not cancel any of that.

So take the day. Rest if you can. Then get up and do one small thing, and let that be enough for today. You are loved from here, loudly and without conditions.

Your brother, Peter.
